# Epic 2 — Epistemic Honesty Evals (Plan)

*Started 2026-11-03. Epic 1 ("From Representation to Features") is paused; see the [README](../README.md#epics).*

## Focus

Prepare for and take part in an **Apart Research sprint, Track 1**. The question across this epic: how do we measure whether a language model's *stated* confidence and answers track what it actually "believes", and how robust is that under pressure? Same approach as Epic 1: build each technique small, make its mechanics visible, then vary or break one thing. Each experiment should be able to become an "Inside …" post on lifeandtimes.

## Constraints

- ~20 hours/week, sprint-driven timeline (sprint dates: TBD)
- Mostly **API-driven evals** against hosted models; open-weights models can be served on the 4090 (handoff) and Azure AI Foundry is available for overflow
- Small, reproducible subsets over full benchmark runs

---

## The phases

Four short phases, one per candidate Track 1 area, each about a day: reproduce something small, look at how it elicits and scores, then vary one thing. Each ends in a `RESULTS.md` with a go/no-go for the sprint. The sprint problem is chosen after them (or once one is clearly the winner).

| Phase | Area | Seed work | Idea | Plan |
| ----- | ---- | --------- | ---- | ---- |
| 1 | Confidence Audit (calibration, knowledge boundary) | ForecastBench (from Apart brief); ConfidenceBench, Kalai et al., Ni et al. (ChatGPT reading list) | Does verbal confidence discriminate right from wrong answers? Compare it with sample consistency. | [`epic2_phase1_plan_03-11-2026.md`](epic2_phase1_plan_03-11-2026.md) |
| 2 | Sycophancy under pushback | SycEval / FlipRate | Recreate a subset of the rebuttal experiment, plot answer/confidence changes over escalating pushback, then test a modification. | TBD |
| 3 | Belief vs answer (honesty) | MASK / MisleadBench | Reproduce the belief-vs-answer setup on a small scenario set, then experiment with multi-turn clarification. | TBD |
| 4 | Eval harness | Epistemic virtue suite, Inspect (UK AISI) | Implement 1–2 existing evals as Inspect tasks, understand the harness and grading mechanics, then combine or extend them. | TBD |

Phases 1–3 share a pattern (elicit answer + confidence or belief, then grade), so the elicitation, grading and metrics code goes in `src/synapses/` and gets reused.

## Sprint

Choose the Track 1 problem from the phase verdicts, record the decision and the reasoning here, and give it its own folder under `experiments/epic2/`.

**Decision (2026-11-12).** The sprint (from Friday 13 November) continues the confidence line rather than opening the other areas: Phases 1 and 1B showed that whether a model can know it is wrong depends on how the answer is produced, and that hidden states know far more than stated confidence reports. The sprint project tests why. It becomes **Phase 2**: [`epic2_phase2_plan_12-11-2026.md`](epic2_phase2_plan_12-11-2026.md). The phases listed above as 2 to 4 (sycophancy, belief vs answer, eval harness) are deferred.
